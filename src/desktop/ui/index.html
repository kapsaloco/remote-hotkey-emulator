How to use
  1. Open this app.
  2. Open your configured emulator and start your game.
  3. First time: choose your PC’s Wi-Fi or Ethernet address in Network settings and set a password (open Command Prompt, type ipconfig, and find the IPv4 Address under your active Wi-Fi or Ethernet adapter).
  4. Click Start server.
  5. On your phone, connect to the same Wi-Fi, open the displayed address, and enter your password.
  6. Choose its profile on your phone, keep the emulator focused on your PC, and use the controls.
